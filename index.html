About the coach

Skating built from the ground up.

True Stride Academy works with players who already play the game — and want to stop losing races to the puck. Every session breaks skating down to its fundamentals: edge control, weight transfer, and stride efficiency, then rebuilds it into speed that holds up in a shift.

Each program is built around where the skater actually is right now — not a generic drill sheet — so every rep serves a clear, specific goal.

What's covered

  • Edge workInside and outside edges, tight turns, crossovers front and back
  • Stride mechanicsPush efficiency, recovery, and posture under speed
  • TransitionsMobility, pivots, and defensive positioning
  • Game speedFirst-step acceleration and explosive starts
Format

1-on-1 & small group

Ice time built around individual stride correction, not a crowded clinic.

Level

Youth through AAA

Programs adapt to the skater — from developing edges to competitive-level speed work.

Approach

Plan, drill, review

Every session runs off a written plan, so progress is visible week to week.
